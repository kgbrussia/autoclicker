Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. General

KGB Autoclicker (package com.kgbrussia.autoclicker) is made by kgbrussia (we, us).

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

2. Accessibility service

The app needs the Android Accessibility Service to perform taps and swipes at coordinates you set.

We do not read screen content from other apps or send it to our servers.

3. Data on your device

  • Settings, target coordinates, and saved scenarios.
  • JSON import/export happens only when you choose a file in the system picker.

Data stays on your device. You can erase it by clearing app data or uninstalling.

4. Notifications and background work

  • A foreground service notification is shown while the overlay panel is active.
  • WAKE_LOCK is used only while a scenario is playing.

Notifications do not contain personal data.

5. Internet and analytics

Core features work offline. There is no ads SDK, billing, or analytics telemetry.

6. Support email

If you email us, we receive your address and message text. We use it only to reply.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
